The client

A Renewed Path is a practice offering psychiatric services and wellness care. They already had a logo and an online booking system in place. What they did not have was a website that pulled it together and gave prospective patients a clear, calm first impression.

What I did

  1. Design and build I designed and built eight pages in Squarespace, working the client's existing logo and booking system into a layout that makes it easy to learn about their services and take the next step.
  2. Content and copy The client supplied most of the page copy. Where they were unsure what to say, I wrote starter copy and guided them through what each page needed, so every page had a clear purpose.
  3. Photography I sourced stock photography that fit the tone of the practice: warm, calm, and welcoming rather than clinical.
  4. Search basics I set up Google Analytics and wrote page titles, meta descriptions, and image alt text for every page, so the site could be found in search and was more accessible to people using screen readers.
  5. Domain and launch The practice needed a new domain. I helped them purchase it, connected it to Squarespace, and launched the site.
