About this project

Free Spirit Outreach is a nonprofit that supports children, the elderly, and people in need through fundraising, outreach events, and scholarships, serving the local community in West Palm Beach and communities in Jamaica.

As a volunteer project coordinator, I owned the magazine from start to print. I collected the pieces from many contributors: letters from dignitaries and the organization's leadership, profiles of each award recipient, chapter updates, the ceremony programme, and sponsor advertisements. I designed every page in Canva, kept the layout consistent across a wide mix of material, and worked with the printer to get the finished issue produced in time for the event.

The magazine was the printed keepsake for the evening. It gave guests the programme, introduced the honorees, and recognized the sponsors who funded the event.
